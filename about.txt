Hi, Tashi Delek. I'm Techung, a Tibetan singer/songwriter living in exile in Dharamshala and New York. I'm known for performing traditional Tibetan music, dance, and opera under the name Tashi Dhondup Sharzur, but I use my childhood nickname, Techung, as a solo artist. My goal is to revive Tibetan music in the Tibetan community and share our rich cultural tradition with the world.

Growing up in Dharamsala, India, I was enrolled in the Tibetan Institute of Performing Arts (TIPA) at age 9, where I studied folk, court, and religious music traditions. I toured with TIPA and later co-founded the Chaksampa Tibetan Dance and Opera Company in 1989, performing at prestigious venues like Carnegie Hall and the Smithsonian Folklife Festival. I've performed with artists like Philip Glass, REM, late Zakir Hussain and other international artists, and my music has been featured in films like IMAX "Everest", "Windhorse", "Mola", "Far From Home" and other documentary films.

I'm a nominee for the National Endowment for the Art's Heritage Award for 2027. I am proud to have received awards like the 2018 New York Foundation for the Arts Fellowship in Folk and Traditional Arts and multiple Tibetan Music Awards. I've performed for His Holiness the Dalai Lama and released albums like "Yarlung: Tibetan Songs of Love and Freedom", "Sky Treasure" and "Techung-Tibet-Lam La Che: On The Road". I'm passionate about preserving Tibetan culture through music, especially for children, with my album "Semshae – Heart Songs".
